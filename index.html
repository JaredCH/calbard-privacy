CalBard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

CalBard is a personal dashboard app for Android TV, tablets, and phones. It shows weather, calendars, sports schedules, and to-dos on a single screen.

This policy explains what CalBard does with your information. The short version: CalBard has no server and no account system. Your settings and saved dashboard data stay on your device. When you use a network feature, the request goes directly to the provider that supplies that data.

Email: Hicksjaredc@gmail.com

2. What CalBard stores on your device

Everything CalBard remembers is stored locally on the device where you installed it. CalBard does not operate a server and does not receive a copy of this data.

Data Why it is stored Where it lives
Your location (ZIP code or latitude/longitude that you type in) To fetch weather for that place App preferences
Calendar feeds you add (ICS URLs) To display your events App preferences
Cached weather, calendar, and sports responses To show data instantly and work offline Local database
Display preferences (theme, layout, which sections are visible) To remember your setup App preferences
To-do items To show your checklist App preferences

When you use the weather section, the place you typed is sent to the weather providers described in section 3 so they can answer the request. It is not sent to a CalBard server.

On Android, any credentials you enter are held in EncryptedSharedPreferences, which is backed by the Android Keystore. On the web build, they are held in the browser's localStorage for the site you loaded. Version 1.3.0 has no Google sign-in, so in practice the only credential-like value stored is the ICS feed URL you paste in.

CalBard does not ask for or store: your name, your contacts, your advertising ID, your precise GPS location, or any analytics or crash telemetry. CalBard does not read your device's GPS; it uses the place you type.

3. Where CalBard sends data

CalBard makes direct requests from your device to the services below. CalBard does not operate a proxy or relay for these requests in the published Android app, and no CalBard server receives them.

Each request necessarily reveals your IP address and a User-Agent string to that service. The service may also log the request — including coordinates or the feed URL — in order to answer it. Those services process the request under their own privacy policies.

Service What is sent Purpose
Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) A place name you typed, or latitude/longitude Weather forecast and geocoding
National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) Latitude/longitude Active weather alerts (US only)
Your calendar host (whatever ICS URL you add) A plain HTTPS GET for the feed Read-only calendar events
AllGameTimes (schedule feeds) A plain HTTPS GET for the public schedule feed of the leagues you follow Sports schedules

The sports section is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings → Display → Sections, and no request is made to AllGameTimes while it is off.

4. Google Calendar data

This section does not apply to version 1.3.0. The Google sign-in flow is not included in this version, so CalBard cannot request a Google scope and never receives Google Calendar data. It is kept here for a future version that adds Google sign-in.

If a later version offers Google sign-in, this is what it would do:

  • CalBard requests the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly. This is read-only. CalBard cannot create, edit, or delete events.
  • CalBard also requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email so it can show you which account is connected.
  • Calendar data is used only to render your dashboard. It is not sent to any third party, not used for advertising, not used to train models, and not sold.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored in encrypted storage on your device.
  • You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the account inside CalBard's settings.

CalBard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data sharing and sale

CalBard does not sell or rent your personal information, and does not share it for advertising. To provide the features you request, CalBard sends the data in section 3 directly to the providers named there. Those providers process it to return the weather, calendar, or sports data; CalBard does not receive a copy.

CalBard does not use a relay for these requests in the published Android app. The optional development proxy in the web build is not deployed in the store app.

6. Children

CalBard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It does not require an account or ask for a name. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the address in section 1.

7. Security

  • All requests the app completes use HTTPS. The Android bridge refuses non-HTTPS URLs, and the web build blocks them as mixed content.
  • Credentials are stored using Android's Keystore-backed encrypted preferences.
  • Android backup is disabled for CalBard, so your credentials are not copied to Google Drive or transferred to a new device.
  • The Android WebView is locked down: file access, content access, and cross-origin file access are all disabled, and mixed content is blocked.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your CalBard data has been compromised, contact the address in section 1.

8. Data retention and deletion

All CalBard data lives on your device and is under your control. There is no CalBard server and no server-side copy.

  • To delete everything: uninstall the app, or use Android's Settings → Apps → CalBard → Storage → Clear data.
  • To delete only cached data: use Android's Settings → Apps → CalBard → Storage → Clear cache. On the web build, clear the site's storage in your browser.
  • To revoke Google access: not applicable in version 1.3.0 — there is no Google sign-in. If a later version adds it, you would remove the account in CalBard's settings and confirm at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be published at the same URL. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
